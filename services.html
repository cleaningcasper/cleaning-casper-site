Services

What we clean

We offer four kinds of cleaning in Brownsville, TX. The lists below describe what each job generally covers. Every property is different, so the exact scope is worked out with you when you request a quote.

  • New construction clean-up
  • Move-in / move-out cleaning
  • Commercial cleaning
  • Airbnb / short-term rental cleaning

New construction clean-up

New construction clean-up is for a space after building or remodeling work. It typically covers removing leftover dust and debris and cleaning surfaces.

Typically includes

  • Removing construction dust from floors, surfaces, and fixtures
  • Picking up leftover debris, scraps, and packaging
  • Wiping counters, cabinets, and other hard surfaces
  • Cleaning kitchen and bathroom areas
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Cleaning interior glass and window sills

Move-in / move-out cleaning

Move-in and move-out cleaning is for a home or unit when someone is moving in or moving out. It is usually done when the rooms are empty or nearly empty.

Typically includes

  • Dusting and wiping surfaces, shelves, and baseboards
  • Cleaning inside empty cabinets, drawers, and closets
  • Cleaning kitchens, including counters, sinks, and appliance exteriors
  • Cleaning bathrooms, including fixtures, sinks, and floors
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Removing leftover dust and debris

Request a quote for Move-in / move-out cleaning

Commercial cleaning

Commercial cleaning is for offices and other business spaces. A visit typically covers routine cleaning of the areas you ask us to handle.

Typically includes

  • Dusting and wiping desks, counters, and other surfaces
  • Cleaning restrooms
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Emptying trash bins
  • Cleaning interior glass at entries and partitions
  • Cleaning break areas and kitchenettes, such as counters and sinks

Request a quote for Commercial cleaning

Airbnb / short-term rental cleaning (turnovers)

Short-term rental cleaning covers turnovers: cleaning the rental between one guest stay and the next.

Typically includes

  • Cleaning kitchens, including counters, sinks, and appliance exteriors
  • Cleaning bathrooms, including fixtures and floors
  • Dusting and wiping surfaces throughout the rental
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Emptying trash
  • Tidying the space between guest stays
